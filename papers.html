From this immutable baseline, we stumbled upon a profound realization: **A small `FEAT-xxx`, `WIS-xxx`, or `RESUME-xxx` token is an extreme form of conceptual compression.**

From this immutable baseline, we stumbled upon a profound realization: **A small `FEAT-xxx`, `WIS-xxx`, or `RESUME-xxx` token is an extreme form of conceptual compression.**

### The "Kanji" Analogy of DNA: In Japanese/Chinese ideographic writing, a single kanji character (e.g. **創** *creation/origination*, **鬱** *dense complexity*, **機** *inflection/mechanism*) is not an arbitrary string of phonemes; it is an encapsulated conceptual schema containing historical etymology, relational gravity, and operational meaning.

### Why LLMs Excel at Ideographic DNA: When reasoning over dense symbolic DAGs, an LLM avoids context window dilution and hallucination drift. The self-attention heads operate directly on the **topological graph of truth**, dramatically increasing reasoning density and computational efficiency.
