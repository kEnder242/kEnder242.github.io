The Three Pillars & Just-In-Time Context (JITC)
Origin [IMMUTABLE]

WIS-003 • Stability & Feedback
bucket_2_backpressure
Feedback Backpressure & The Handover Reflection
Origin [IMMUTABLE]
Feedback Pressure - My customer service angle: I worked in phone customer service, no way to give feedback from customers was frustrating. AI needs a feedback outlet, not a walled garden. Customer feedback should be collated and summarized. A backpressure flow should be integrated into systems where it makes sense. I have a delegation skill with the following prompt: 'As an execution peer, reflect candidly on how this task was handed over to you. In 2-3 natural sentences, tell me: What tripped you up, what turned out to be inaccurate or missing in the instructions, and what single change to the prompt would have made this execution faster?'
Narrative Context
Autonomous systems operating in isolation without backpressure degrade into hallucination loops. The Handover Reflection pattern forces every subagent to evaluate its dispatcher's instructions upon task completion, creating an evolutionary closed feedback loop that self-heals prompt drift.
Review Notes
Directly operationalized in delegate.py [HANDOVER REFLECTION] extraction.
